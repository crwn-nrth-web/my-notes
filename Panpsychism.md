> [How do you explain consciousness? | David Chalmers Ted Talk](https://youtu.be/uhRhtFFhNzQ?si=FJ6nyjh5CE7YnjFJ)

Consciousness is a datum that needs to be explained; we should not reduce it or explain it away. Instead, consciousness needs to be treated as a genuine [[Kuhn-on-crisis-and-scientific-revolutions|anomaly]]. 

Chalmers offers a view of consciousness =
- Consciousness is fundamental (in the same way space and time is considered as fundamental)
- Consciousness is universal = **panpsychism** (every system has consciousness)
