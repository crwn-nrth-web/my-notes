classifying objects based on:
1. brightness change over time (light curve)
2. periods of changes (folded light curve with the period)
3. color (color-magnitude diagram)

**Eclipsing Binary (ECL)**
- class of variables consisting of pair of stars revolving around their common center of mass in an orbit that we can detect when they eclipse each other

**Cepheid (CEP)**
- a class of pulsating stars that periodically expand and contract, typically changing their brightness over a period of a few days.

**RR Lyrae (RR)**
- a class of pulsating stars which periodically expand and contract, typically changing their brightness over a period in the range between 0.2 and 1 day.

**Long period variable (LPV)**
- a class of pulsating stars which have already evolved to the phase of red giants or supergiants.
