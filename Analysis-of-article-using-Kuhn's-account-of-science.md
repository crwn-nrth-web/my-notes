 > ***Source:*** Jorryt Matthee, Black holes disguised as little red dots, _Science_ **391**, 767-768 (2026). DOI:[10.1126/science.adz8603](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adz8603)

#### Summary of Article
- one of JWST's discoveries generated intense debate
- red and compact astronomical objects, nicknames "little red dots" commonly found with the JWST despite the telescope's small field of view
- thought to be powered by the gravitational energy released by materials falling into a supermassive black hole
- the abundance and unusual properties of the little red dots could potentially unveil a new phase in the emergence of supermassive black holes


The article discusses evidence for a suspected previously unidentified early phase of supermassive black hole growth, where black holes appear as faint "little red dots" in deep-space observations by the James Webbs Space Telescope (JWST). The objects don't fit neatly into existing categories of galaxies or active galactic nuclei
