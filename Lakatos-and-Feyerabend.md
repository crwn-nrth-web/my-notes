 **Lakatos** was a student of [[Popper-theory-of-scientific-change]] and accused [[Kuhn-1962-structure-of-scientific-revolutions]]  reducing science to *mob psychology* (because [[Kuhn-on-normal-science|normal-science]] was based on consensus and had heavy subjective elements)

#### Lakatos' account of science

- A healthy scientific field contains several competing research programs  
- Scientists in each program defend their views against falsification  
- There are good and bad ways to do this  
- Programs that always do it badly are eventually abandoned

Lakatos's idea of "research program" is a rival idea to [[Kuhn-on-paradigms]].

This is a modification of Popper, who wanted to falsify everything. For Lakatos, falsification is still the primary basis but the focus is falsifying other research programs in order to support your research program.

There is also an evolution basis in Lakatos account that as scientists choose their preferred research program and one research programs become more popular, unpopular research programs die out.

- A ‘progressive’ research program responds to falsification by amending the theory in a way that leads to new  discoveries (‘progressive problem-shift’)  
- A ‘degenerating’ research program defends itself in a way that leads to no new discoveries (‘degenerative problem-shift’)
