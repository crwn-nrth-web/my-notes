Marino is being skeptical about the idea that getting right the foundations of mathematics will help fix problems with formalization and mathematization of economics

Marino supports the idea that mathematics is mind-dependent. This relates to [[Maddy-2008-How-applied-maths-became-pure]] story that we are setting up definitions and seeing what follows, as well defining the logics. The fact that we are setting up the definitions is what makes the mathematics mind-dependent.

Whether an idealization works or is it useful is contextual

**mathematization of economics** = focused on various theorems about if an agent has these certain axioms within certain conditions, then you will get a permutable outcome

**Hilbert's version of formalism** = 
- mathematical systems as abstract networks of logical relations where basic terms do not need intuitive content
- objects like infinity are simply useful yet meaningless symbols manipulated according to strict formal rules, much like pieces in a game
- treating mathematics as simply moving around symbols

**Brouwer intuitionism** =
- views mathematics not as the discovery of an external, pre-existing reality, but as a languageless mental creation constructed step-by-step through pure intuition.  
- rejects the classic *law of the excluded middle* that every statement is either true or false. for an intuitionalist, a statement is true only if you have a constructive proof of it, and false only if you have a proof that it leads to a contradiction.
- uses intuitionistic logic where truth equals having a direct proof
