Dennett doesn't buy into the Chalmer's [[hard-problem-of-consciousness-and-the-explanatory gap|hard-problem-of-consciousness]] as he thinks the question is based on the implicit assumption: that there is a subject inside the mind-brain that is enjoying the quale. It is also implicitly assumed that there is a stage or theatre where this all comes together for the subject: where qualia are presented to the subject like a film is presented to a viewer in a theatre.
