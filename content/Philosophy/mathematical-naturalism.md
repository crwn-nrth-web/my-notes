The general idea behind naturalism is that all of our legitimate knowledge is acquired in a scientific way. Mathematical naturalism treats mathematics as a part of the natural world and of human practice, not as a realm of abstract objects known by special intuition.

## How are Kitcher and Maddy Naturalists?

Kitcher and Maddy are focused on two different questions:
- *Kitcher* = where does mathematical knowledge come from, and how does it develop rationally? 
	- Elementary mathematics originates in interactions with the physical world, and later the practice grows from that through rational transitions
- *Maddy* = by what standards is mathematics now evaluated, and does application make it answerable to empirical confirmation?
	- Mathematical methodology is autonomous, even for applied mathematics
