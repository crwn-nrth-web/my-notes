> McCluskey, S. C. (1993). Different Cultures, Different Cosmologies. In N. S. Hetherington (Ed.), _Cosmology: Historical, Literary, Philosophical, Religious and Scientific Perspectives_. Garland Publishing.

In *Different cultures, Different Cosmologies*, Stephen C. McCluskey points out that the fact that different cultures produced different cosmologies suggests that **science is relative** and that scientific knowledge "can be understood as a product of "interests" with its categories and criteria of truth determined by cultural and social requirements."
