First proposed by Plato, the task of [[Ancient-astronomy]] working in the Platonic tradition was to find some combination of uniform circular motions in agreement with the observed irregular motion of the planets. Combining with Aristotle's dynamic model of the universe, *saving the phenomena* came to mean accounting for the motions of the planets while adhering to Aristotelian principles.

To modern times, "saving the phenomena" seems to be an absurd and arbitrary task. But it is important to understand that the Greeks had concerns and values much different from our own. 

They saw the planets as unchanging from generations to generations. So they believed that the planets must be made of a changeless, incorruptible substance (*aether*) and followed the only changeless motion: uniform circular motion
