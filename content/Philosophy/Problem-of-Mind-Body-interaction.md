This is the challenge of explaining how non-physical mental events can be related to and interact with the physical body.

**Substance Dualism's take** =

![[Descartes-Meditations#^0f73cf]]

Modern science assumes that everything in the physical world can be explained by physical laws; no scientific laws refer to non-physical / mental causes. [[Descartes-Meditations|Descartes-substance-Dualism]] leads to a conflict with physical science: if the mind has no physical properties, how could it cause physical changes in the body? [[Descartes-Meditations|Descartes-substance-Dualism]] violates and is incompatible with the idea that universe operates according to consistent, lawful, physical principles. Because of this, [[Armstrong-materialist-view-of-the-mind]] and [[Mind-Brain-Identity-theory]] finds an alternative explanation of what minds are based on a scientific approach.
