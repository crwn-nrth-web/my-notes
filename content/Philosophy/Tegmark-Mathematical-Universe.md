**Assumptions**
- **External Reality Hypothesis** (ERH) = “there exists an external physical reality completely independent of us humans.” (Tegmark, 2008, p. 1)
- **Mathematical Universe Hypothesis** (MUH) = “our physical world is an abstract mathematical structure.” (Tegmark, 2008, p. 1)
- **scientific realism** = "physics theories aim to describe how this assumed external reality *works*" (p. 1)
- **reductionism** = hierarchical relations of theories 

> “physics has come to focus on the way the external reality works (described by regularities known as laws of physics) rather than on the way it is (the subject of initial conditions).” (Tegmark, 2008, p. 2)

**Thesis** = Tegmark argues that with a broad definition of mathematics, the External Reality Hypothesis implies the Mathematical Universe Hypothesis. 

**Tegmark's Pythagoreanism** = "our universe is mathematics in a well-defined sense" (p. 1)
- he does not endorse the anthropocentric view of mathematics that [[Steiner-on-the-applicability-of-mathematics]] endorses
- it makes sense that mathematics describes our physical theories because the world is maths

Tegmark also argues for **complete mathematical democracy** = "that mathematical existence and physical existence are equivalent, so that all mathematical structures have the same ontological status.” (Tegmark, 2008, p. 16) 
- you shouldn't think that mathematical structures are created, but discovered  
- argument against: what does it mean if we develop wrong math?

*Implications of ERH* =
> “The ERH implies that for a description to be complete, it must be well-defined also according to non-human sentient entities (say aliens or future supercomputers) that lack the common understanding of concepts that we humans have evolved, e.g., “particle”, “observation” or indeed any other English words. Put differently, such a description must be expressible in a form that is devoid of human “baggage”.” (Tegmark, 2008, p. 1)

For Tegmark, our theories can be "crudely organized" in a family tree where each might be derivable from more fundamental ones above it. Our theories have two components:
1. mathematical equations
	- **Mathematical structure** = "abstract entities with relations between them" (p. 2)
2. **baggage** = "words that explain how they are connected to what we humans observe and intuitively understood" (p. 1)

> “Crudely speaking, the ratio of equations to baggage decreases as we move down the tree, dropping near zero for highly applied fields such as medicine and sociology. In contrast, theories near the top are highly mathematical . . . the MUH implies that the Theory of Everything is purely mathematical, with no baggage whatsoever” (Tegmark, 2008, p. 2)

### what it means that the world "is" math?

Tegmark explains:
> "the customary terminology in physics textbooks is that the external reality is described by mathematics, the MUH states that it is mathematics. . .  If a future physics textbook contains the Theory of Everything, then its equations are the complete description of the mathematical structure that is the external physical reality. . . .If there is an isomorphism between a mathematical structure and another structure (a one-to-one correspondence between the two that respects the relations), then they are one and the same. If our external physical reality is isomorphic to a mathematical structure, it therefore fits the definition of being a mathematical structure." (Tegmark, 2008, p. 4)

**What is math?**
> “A mathematical structure S (defined and illustrated with examples in Appendix A) is essentially a collection of abstract entities with relations (functions) between them” (Tegmark, 2008, p. 5)

**isomorphism** = structure-preserving mapping between two mathematical structures that can be reversed by inverse mapping

**automorphism** = a structure-preserving map (an isomorphism) from a mathematical object or set to itself

### The Frogs and the Birds (p. 3)
Frog = observer living concretely in the world (with all the baggage)
Bird = mathematician studying the mathematical structure

> “In the four-dimensional spacetime of the bird perspective, these particle trajectories resemble a tangle of spaghetti. If the frog sees a particle moving with constant velocity, the bird sees a straight strand of uncooked spaghetti. If the frog sees a pair of orbiting particles, the bird sees two spaghetti strands intertwined like a double helix. To the frog, the world is described by Newton’s laws of motion and gravitation. To the bird, it is described by the geometry of the pasta, obeying the mathematical relations corresponding to minimizing the Newtonian action.” (Tegmark, 2008, p. 3)

We can presumably never have the bird's perspective.

If the Frog is only able to do empirical observations (using its baggage), then how can the Bird's theory ever be confirmed? So perhaps, the Frog's perspective is usefulness and we should not try to reduce the baggage that Tegmark is arguing for.

### Parallel Universes

Distinguishing between different types of parallel universes:
1. *Level 1* = same laws of physics, different initial conditions
2. *Level 2* = same fundamental laws of physics, different constants, particles, dimensionality
3. *Level 3* = different fundamental equations of physics
4. *Level 4* = other mathematical structures give different fundamental equations of physics for the Theory of Everything
