Carlo Rovelli talks about how the separation between philosophy and physics is not only artificial but damaging for both discipline. He talks about how Einstein, maxwell required a critical thinking that philosophers possess to rethink the discipline in order to solve problems of physics.

*is there any open problems of physics that you think would benefit philosophical thinking* = one instance is time. in trying to write a quantum theory of gravity, space and time have to change because now we're looking at the quantum property of space and time and we have so many prejudices about how space should do should be and how time should be, where the critical and radical thinking of philosophers come in handy.
