## Main points 

#### 2.4: expanison in the universe 
- **Hubble's law** = the velocity of recession was proportional to the distance of an object, i.e. the further away from us a galaxy is, the faster it is receding. $v = H_0 d$
- the velocites are measured using **redshift** = the Doppler effect applied to light waves. If the galaxy is receding, the characteristic absorption and emission lines move towards the red end of the spectrum and the effect is known as a redshift.  $z = v/c$

#### 3.4 Friedmann equations
- **Newton's theorem** = in a spherically-symmetric distribution of matter, a particle feels no force at all from the material at greater radii, and the material at smaller radii gives exactly the force which one would get if all the material was concentrated at the central point
