Kuhn believes that we need to give up the old paradigm completely when we gain a new paradigm as they cannot translate together.

Whats going on nowadays, when we give up the "old paradigm" we sometimes still use the old paradigm in limited applications. For example, quantum theory is the new paradigm currently, but we haven't thrown away the old paradigm (Newtonian physics)
