If the universe's physical constants or attributes (the force that holds particles together, gravity, the speed of light, etc.) were even just a little bit different, there would be a universe where stars never formed, or matter didn't exist. The attributes of the universe seem to be fine-tuned to form a universe that would end up having stars, planets, the periodic table of elements, etc.

This implies =
- there are an infinite number of universes with different attributes, and we are in the one universe that has the specific attributes that allow us to exist
- or, there is someone, something, some system that set the initial conditions for the universe to exist in this way
- or, there is a physical explanation (that we don't know yet) of why the attributes of the universe are so fine-tuned
