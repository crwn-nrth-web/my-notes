**Double slits experiment** = When electrons are fired through two slits, it goes against the classical picture and an interference pattern is seen. Quantum mechanically, the interference pattern shows the variation of probability, i.e. 
$$
P(x,t) \propto |\psi (x,t)|^2 = \psi^{*} (x,t) \psi (x,t)
$$
Formally, probability of detecting a particle within volume $dr = dx dy dz$  centered at $r = (x, y , z)$ at t = $P(r,t) dr = |\psi (r,t)|^2$
