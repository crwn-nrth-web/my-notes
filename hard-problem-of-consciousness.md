David Chalmers and Joseph Levine 

*Why is there something it is like to be conscious? Why do physical processes in the brain give rise to subjective experience?*  = these are not the same questions posed by [[Nagel-what-is-it-like-to-be]] and [[Jackson-what-Mary-didn't-know]]. These questions are explanatory questions; even if we could explain "what it is like", we wouldn't be able to answer the **explanatory gap**

**easy problem of conciousness** = phenomena we can give an explanation of

**hard problem of consciousness**
